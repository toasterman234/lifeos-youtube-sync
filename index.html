lifeos youtube sync

lifeos youtube sync is a personal, single-user tool. It uses the YouTube Data API (read-only access) to copy the account owner's own YouTube liked videos and channel subscriptions into files stored on the owner's own computer, so the owner can keep a local, searchable record of their YouTube activity alongside their watch history.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users. It never modifies anything on YouTube.
