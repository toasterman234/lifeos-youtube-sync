Privacy Policy — lifeos youtube sync

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

lifeos youtube sync is a personal, single-user application operated by its owner for the owner's own YouTube account. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.readonly), the app reads the owner's own YouTube liked videos and channel subscriptions through the YouTube Data API. It does not read any other person's data, and it cannot change anything on YouTube.

Where the data goes

The data is stored only in files on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared, uploaded, or transmitted to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access stops all future reads; the locally stored files remain under the owner's control and can be deleted by the owner at any time.
